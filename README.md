# EV_Sales_Dashboard
**Dashboard Overview**
The main objective of this Power BI project is to analyze EV sales data and generate meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations.
**Objective**
The main objective of this Power BI project is to analyze EV sales data and generate meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations.
**The dashboard aims to:**
Monitor overall EV sales and revenue performance.
Identify the top and bottom-performing manufacturers.
Analyze revenue trends across different years.
Compare sales performance across regions.
Understand quarterly unit sales patterns.
Analyze revenue contribution from different customer segments.
Provide interactive filtering for better business decision-making.
**Conclusion**
The EV Sales Dashboard successfully transforms raw EV sales data into an interactive and easy-to-understand business intelligence solution. The analysis shows strong revenue performance, with 2025 being the highest-performing year and Tata Motors leading among the manufacturers. The dashboard also highlights differences in regional, quarterly, and customer-segment performance.
